## 10-minute limit on local verification (U22)

The rule, binding on both workers: every local command (test suite, coverage run, benchmark, image build, Playwright or browser check) finishes in under 10 minutes of wall-clock time; any that could run long is wrapped in `timeout 600`; a check that cannot fit is split into targeted pieces that each fit, or left to CI and recorded in the run's `closure.md` with the reason; never sit waiting on a running command or on a lease with nothing else to do — keep working in parallel or move on; a timeout is a finding to fix by narrowing the scope, never a reason to raise the limit or weaken the check. Recipe: `docs/state/context/test-environment.md`, «10-minute limit on every local command».
